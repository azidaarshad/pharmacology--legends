NSNT LEGENDS — AUTO MARK FIX V4

SEQ/Clinical answers are now scored and saved automatically when submitted.
- No manual GM MARK is required.
- MCQ: correct = individual +1, team +1; wrong = individual 0, team -1.
- SEQ/Clinical: deterministic automatic score 0-100 is calculated from the reference answer/rubric text.
- >=50: individual +1, team +1.
- <50: individual 0, team -1.
- The score, individual point, team point and AUTO mark are saved in D1 immediately.
- GM Control Room displays Auto instead of a manual MARK button.
- Existing /api/mark-open endpoint is retained for compatibility but is no longer exposed in the UI.
